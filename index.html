Kayla Carabes — Painter & Multidisciplinary Artist

Kayla Carabes is a multidisciplinary artist (b. 2002, Houston, TX) working across painting and performance, focused on natural environments as sites of memory, transformation, and storytelling. She is earning a BFA in Painting at the Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) in Providence, RI. Browse original oil paintings, limited-edition prints, and crafts available to purchase.
